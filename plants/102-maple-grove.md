A stand of small maples planted together in one tray to resemble a wood seen from a great distance. In autumn they turned the red of a magistrate's robe, and visitors were made to admire them. Maples drink without pause and forgive nothing. The leaves crisped first, then the twigs, and the wood was a wood no longer.
