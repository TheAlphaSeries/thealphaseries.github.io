A grove of coast redwoods, a tree that left to itself lives two thousand years and stands taller than a cathedral. These were kept a foot high in a dish. They are creatures of the fog and take their water from the air as much as the soil; deprived of both they went the color of rust in a fortnight. Their wild cousins a few miles north did not notice.
