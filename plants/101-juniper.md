Two low green junipers, the customary first trees of every beginner and the customary first losses. They are hardy to the point of insolence and will endure frost, wind and neglect of nearly every kind. The exception is a missed watering in warm weather, of which they do not complain: they simply remain green for several weeks after they have died, out of spite.
