In these latter days, when the old red sun gutters like a lamp short of oil and may at any hour go dark, a prudent man keeps account of what he has taken from the world before the world is done with him.

Herein are set down the denizens of the cold lakes of California, each drawn forth by rod, line and low cunning. The weights are exact. As to the rest, the compiler entertains no disputes and offers no refunds.
