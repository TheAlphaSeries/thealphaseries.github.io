A small round personage of the shallows, gilded like a coin and very nearly as common. It guards its patch of gravel with a ferocity out of all proportion to its size, and will rush at intruders many times larger: a policy that philosophers have called valiant and fishermen have called convenient.

Along the lakeshore it is agreed that to catch one confers no distinction, and that to catch none is a disgrace.
