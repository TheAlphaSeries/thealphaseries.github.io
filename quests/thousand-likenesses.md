The keeper owns a dark box that eats light and gives back likenesses, and has for some seasons fed it faithfully while showing its work to no one. The savants hold that a likeness unseen is no likeness at all, merely a rumour kept in a drawer. He means to settle the matter by producing a thousand of them and letting the public judge, a tribunal he has so far avoided.
