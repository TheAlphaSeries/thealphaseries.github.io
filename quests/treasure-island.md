A small but exceptionally capable company is to be assembled and sail for Treasure Island. The purpose is singular and culturally significant: to capture the finest photograph yet made of the San Francisco skyline.

Two or three men are required, of various builds, temperaments and useful dispositions: a land surveyor, a pack hauler, and a charming fellow to secure passage through ports, gates, checkpoints, and whatever minor authorities presume to stand between the company and destiny. The post of mystic is filled. The keeper will consult the heavens, interpret the light, and point toward the horizon as required.

Those who would join should choose a post below and petition for it. If all perform their duties admirably, glory is probable.
