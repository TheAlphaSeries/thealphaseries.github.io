Home. A city of forty-odd hills at the end of a peninsula, wrapped most mornings in a fog so thick that the residents have given it a name and speak of it as a neighbor. The streets were laid out by a man with a ruler and no regard for the terrain, so that they proceed directly up slopes a goat would decline.

The Mission, in the sunny lee of the hills, is where this log is kept. It is older than the city around it, loud at all hours, painted on every available wall, and warmer than the rest by a margin its inhabitants will mention unprompted.
