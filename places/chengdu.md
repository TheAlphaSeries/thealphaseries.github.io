A city of teahouses, peppercorns and deliberate idleness, where the citizens have raised the afternoon nap to a civic art. The cuisine is designed to numb the mouth so that the visitor cannot complain of the heat, and the district's most celebrated residents are black and white, eat only bamboo, and decline all interviews.
